### About

Software engineer and founder. I build products end to end, from the first design frame to the store release, and increasingly I build the AI that runs inside them.

I graduated in Software Engineering from **ESPRIT** (Tunis) after a Bachelor's from **ISIG Kairouan**. I have shipped apps to the App Store and Google Play, run a platform in production, and placed 1st, 3rd and 4th across five hackathons. Most of it solo, most of it across the whole stack.

Arabic, French, English.

---

### What I'm building

| Project | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **[Owemie](https://www.owemie.com)** | Personal finance app for tracking money between people. Reminders, proof photos, PDF statements, shared ledgers. | Live on iOS + Android |
| **[Ömrüm](https://omrum.space)** | A private app for two. No signup, just a pairing code. Lock-screen presence, live shared drawing, calls. | Live on the App Store |
| **Weevia** | Trust-first community platform for immigrants and newcomers in Brazil. Self-hosted face verification for KYC, hybrid content moderation. | Built, ran in production, decommissioned |
| **[Learn2Link](https://learn2link.tn)** | Learning and internship-matching platform with live video. | Live + on Google Play |
| **[El Benna El Tounsia](https://elbenna-el-tounsia.vercel.app)** | Restaurant site built as an interactive flip-book menu. | Live |

Plus a private AI assistant that runs entirely on my own machine and learns from my corrections. Local models, vector memory, fine-tuning on the GPU. It is the project I am most excited about and it is never finished.
